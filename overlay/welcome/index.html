What is a pod?

  • A locker on the webYour notes, files and settings are kept here, not inside each app.
  • Your name on the webYour WebID is the address that says who you are when an app asks.
  • You hand out the keysAn app reaches your pod only after you say yes, here, on this server.

This server runs the Community Solid Server, an open implementation of the Solid protocol.
